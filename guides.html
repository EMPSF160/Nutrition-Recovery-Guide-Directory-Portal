Common Questions

Clinical Recovery FAQs

How does NOURISHMAP verify provider credentials?

Every dietitian and physician on our directory must submit verifiable primary source credentials (CDR Registered Dietitian license, state board licensure, or board certifications like CNS, CSO, or CEDRD). Our clinical audit team checks these against public state databases every 90 days.

Can I receive insurance reimbursement for clinical nutrition therapy?

Many specialists on NOURISHMAP accept major private health insurance (Aetna, BlueCross, UnitedHealthcare) or provide standardized Superbills with ICD-10 & CPT medical nutrition codes for out-of-network claims.

What is the difference between a health coach and a Clinical Dietitian (RD)?

Registered Dietitians (RD/RDN) and Certified Nutrition Specialists (CNS) possess accredited graduate medical nutrition education, 1,000+ hours of supervised clinical residency, and are legally licensed to prescribe Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT) for complex diseases.
